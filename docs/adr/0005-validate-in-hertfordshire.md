# Validate routing quality in Hertfordshire first

Spokes will tune first on Hertfordshire data and use 20 inspectable Hertfordshire journeys as its initial quality gate: 12 Rider-chosen destinations from a shared Welwyn Garden City start and eight deliberately varied cases covering different geographic barriers and network conditions. At least 16 must be usable after Rider review and the Least-Traffic route must beat CycleStreets Quietest on estimated motor exposure for at least 16; Explicit Exclusions are never eligible and all ambiguous passage must be marked Unverified.
